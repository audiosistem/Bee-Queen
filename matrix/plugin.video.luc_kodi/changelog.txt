  * PIN flow authorization (no client_secret, simkl.com/pin)
  * Real-time scrobbling: /scrobble/start on play, /scrobble/pause during playback, /scrobble/stop at 80% to auto-mark watched

[B][COLOR ff00fa9a]Version 1.0.02[/COLOR][/B]
-A new, intelligent feature is available that learns over time how we interact with the plugin, thus improving the "For You" experience. This involves learning, for example, which resolution (4K, 1080p, 720p, etc.) we watch most often, which debrid service we prefer (Premiumize, All Debrid, Real Debrid, etc.), and which color dynamic range we like best (HDR, Dolby Vision, SDR, etc.). I've called this feature "Source Ranker." It requires at least 10 playbacks, ideally 30 or more, to learn our preferences. These preferences can also be reset and viewed statically in the plugin settings.
