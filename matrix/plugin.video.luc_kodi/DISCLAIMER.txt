luc_kodi - Disclaimer
=====================

This add-on is a front-end (a user interface) for third-party services that you
configure with your own accounts: debrid services (such as Real-Debrid,
AllDebrid, Premiumize, TorBox) and torrent indexers.

This add-on hosts no content and includes no links. It does not host, store,
upload, stream or distribute any media, and it does not embed or include any
links or magnets to content. Every source is located and resolved at run time by
the third-party services you have configured with your own credentials. Without
your own accounts and configuration, the add-on returns no playable sources.

You are responsible for the sources you choose to enable and for complying with
the laws of your country, including copyright law. The author takes no
responsibility for the content third-party services return, or for how the
add-on is used. Use at your own risk.
